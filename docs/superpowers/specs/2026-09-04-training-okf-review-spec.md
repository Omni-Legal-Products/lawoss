## Deliverables

- A dated research note with source links, access date, what was observed, and what is an inference.
- An updated LAWOSS usage guide containing only original operational guidance derived from the reviewed material.
- An OKF review note containing the actual D1–D9/O1/O2/O6 topics, risks, recommendations, and explicit team decision fields.
- No automatic merge, approval, public comment, or architectural decision on behalf of the team.

## Quality bar

- Prefer primary/official sources and link each material claim.
- Distinguish observed source content, LAWOSS-specific adaptation, and unresolved team decisions.
- Do not copy course content or reproduce long source passages.
- Keep legal confidentiality, least privilege, review-before-write, and reproducibility visible in the recommendation.
